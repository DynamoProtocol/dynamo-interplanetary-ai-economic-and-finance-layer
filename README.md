Dynamo Protocol is building the Interplanetary AI Economic and Finance Layer—infrastructure that helps AI agents coordinate work, manage shared budgets, and pay for services.

Dynamo connects metered usage to programmable payments, with capped allowances, revocable access, and verifiable records of service delivery. Developers can use it to structure workflows across AI inference, compute, APIs, and data services. Control Mode supports budget enforcement and usage metering without moving money.

Its long-term vision extends across borders and across space: enabling an AI economy that connects agents, services, and future space-based infrastructure.

One enforceable budget. Many agents. An interplanetary vision.
